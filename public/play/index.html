How to play

Sort every envelope before the line backs up. Here is everything you need for a 45-second round.

  1. Each round lasts 45 seconds.
  2. Envelopes line up at the red postbox. Up to 6 can wait — if the line overflows, the post office closes and the round ends. During Fever, extra arrivals are skipped.
  3. Check the stamp on the first envelope, then tap the postbox button with the same color and shape: flower, moon, or star.
  4. Every matching envelope in a row at the front goes out together. A batch of n envelopes scores n(n+1)/2 points.
  5. A wrong tap costs 2 points. Your score never drops below 0.
  • Gold letters match any stamp and double the batch score. The 4th consecutive correct stamp scores ×2 and the 8th scores ×3. A wrong stamp or 3.5 seconds with mail waiting breaks the combo.
  • Send 18 letters to start 6 seconds of Fever. Points double and extra arrivals are skipped when the queue is full.
  • Earn stars at 150, 300, and 450 points.
